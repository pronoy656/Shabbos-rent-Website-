# Landing Page Requirements

## 1. Overview
The landing page should be designed to be beautiful, engaging, and dynamic. Currently, it focuses primarily on users. We need to maintain this focus but expand the scope to build trust and explicitly include value propositions for providers.

## 2. User Focus (Trust & Security)
- **Trust Building**: Emphasize that users are safe and secure with our platform.
- **Future-Proof**: Communicate clearly that users are "future-proof" with us, ensuring their long-term security and reliability.

## 3. Provider Focus (Partnership & Growth)
- **Importance of Providers**: Elevate the importance of providers on the landing page so they feel valued alongside users.
- **Future-Proof for Providers**: Emphasize that providers are also future-proof.
- **Partnership & Business Expansion**: Highlight that providers are considered partners. Joining the platform allows them to expand their business plans, operate independently, and significantly increase their income sources.

## 4. Design & Aesthetics
- Ensure the landing page is visually appealing, incorporating modern design aesthetics to highlight these new characteristics for both users and providers.
